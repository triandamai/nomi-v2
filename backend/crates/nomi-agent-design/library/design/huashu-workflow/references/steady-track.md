# The steady track: when unsure, start from something that already works

Use this track when what you made doesn't hold up (the same problems survive two rounds of fixes, a polish round finds the whole page mediocre), when you are short of context or can't look at your own renders, or when time is very short. The rule is one line: **never start from a blank page; every step edits something that is already done well.** It won't produce the most striking work, but it won't produce the "beige ground, three cards, nothing else" draft either. The direction gate, the asset protocol and the readability floors still apply.

## 1. Candidates from proven starting points

Each of the six direction candidates lands on a concrete starting point: a style specimen (`huashu-design-styles`), a Taste style in `styles/`, or a reference you actually looked at. A "best designer" candidate names the specimen closest to that designer's temperament. A real-world reference you could not see is written as "not seen, starting from specimen X". The six starting points must differ from each other: not six dark ones, not six big-type ones.

## 2. First screen: make the starting point this project's

Keep its skeleton, size relationships and temperament. Replace three things, none may survive from the original:

- **Words:** this project's real headline and content.
- **Colour:** re-derived from the subject (`huashu-design-styles`, colour protocol).
- **Hero visual:** something from this subject's world instead of the specimen's planet, record or mountain.

## 3. The whole page: write a screen-by-screen outline first

The common failure: the first screen is designed, everything below is "small label, big sentence" or "heading, three identical cards" to the end. Before drawing, write one row per screen:

| Screen | Which content | Which form | The hero of this screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One-line position | First screen | Cross-section of the coffee terraces at four altitudes |
| 2 | Why it exists | Story | "Tiga generasi" and the year 1974 |
| 3 | How it's made | Before / after | 30 days drying vs 7 days |

Three rules: every piece of content the person gave lands on some screen; **two neighbouring screens never use the same form**; every screen's hero can be named (if not, it isn't designed yet).

Forms to choose from, by content, not in order:

| Form | Good for | How |
|---|---|---|
| Product UI with callouts | What a feature looks like | Draw the interface, two or three thin leader lines |
| Playable demo | The core mechanism | One slider or toggle; the picture changes (annotate for Koda) |
| Data as image | One key number | Draw it as length, area, density, never a number card |
| Before / after | Old vs new | Two identical frames, one difference |
| Section or map | Space, levels, routes | One full-width drawing, labels on it |
| Timeline | A process, a day, 48 hours | Horizontal scale, events hung on it |
| Big statement | The single most important sentence | One sentence, so large it becomes the picture |
| Story | Origin, who made it | Narrow column, generous leading, one photo or year |
| Image and text | One feature explained | Half picture, half text; swap sides on the next |
| List or spec table | Coverage, specs, branches | Hairline table or two columns, no cards |
| Price comparison | Plans | Columns side by side, differences aligned row by row |
| Closing action | Order, contact | One action, space around it |

A landing page normally uses five or six forms. Keep the first screen's type ladder (four or five sizes), colours, lines and spacing on every screen.

Landing-page checklist: first screen has one sentence, the hero material and one main action; the product is visible (an app page shows the app's screens drawn with real content); each feature told in a different form; every piece of the person's content placed, nothing invented; every screen has at least one picture or one big type moment; the 390 frame doesn't break and body text is at least 16px.

## 4. Safe colour and type

- Colour: two or three hues plus a neutral set, all taken from the subject, with one sentence saying why. If you can't write the sentence you are copying a recipe.
- Type: one pairing from the tables (`taste-type` for sites, `huashu-typography` for decks and video). Display and body, never a third face.
- When unsure, more contrast is safer: headline at least 2.5 times the body size.

## 5. Before handing over

Run the polish loop (`huashu-polish-loop`) as usual, and say honestly which steps followed this track and which were skipped ("belum dicek lewat tampilan render").
