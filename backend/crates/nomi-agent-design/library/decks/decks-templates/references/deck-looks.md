# Deck looks

A catalogue of named deck looks you can offer as directions, grouped by feel, each with the occasion it suits, its palette roles, type pairing, signature move and what to flatten for the canvas.

## How to choose

1. Start from the occasion and the audience's formality (a bank or a ministry: high; a bazaar or a class of kids: low).
2. Offer two or three looks from different groups, with one line each on why they fit. Never mix two looks in one deck.
3. The person's brand colour replaces the look's accent; keep its paper and ink. A look with a fixed preset palette (the paper-and-ink and Swiss ones) swaps only the accent, never the paper.
4. Map colours to tokens: paper `$color-background`, ink `$color-foreground`, secondary `$color-muted-foreground`, the accent `$color-primary`, extra look colours as `$color-chart-1…5` or custom pairs.
5. Flatten: gradients, glows, scanlines, grain, blurred blobs and gradient text become the base solid colour plus a build note. Neon is never a fill.
6. Fonts: name the real font; the Studio may show a stand-in.

Format: **Name** · occasion · palette (roles) · type · signature move.

## Paper and ink (editorial, calm, story-led)

Preset palettes, one per deck, swap only the accent.

- **Ink Classic** · talks, launches, the safe default · paper #F1EFEA, ink #0A0A0B · serif display + sans body + mono meta · huge serif titles, no boxes, no shadows, structure from type size and space; a faint giant numeral behind chapter slides.
- **Indigo Porcelain** · research, data, tech talks · paper #F1F3F5, ink #0A1F3D · same trio · cool and precise.
- **Forest Ink** · nature, sustainability, culture, F&B with a story · paper #F5F1E8, ink #1A2E1F · same trio.
- **Kraft Paper** · heritage, craft, literary, a batik or kopi origin story · paper #EEDFC7, ink #2A1E13 · same trio · warm, nostalgic.
- **Dune** · art, design, fashion · paper #F0E6D2, ink #1F1A14 · same trio.
- **Kami parchment** · company profile, lab or research deck, quiet premium · parchment #F5F4ED content, ink-blue #1B365D cover/chapter/end slides · one serif at one weight, no italics, no cool greys · ink-blue only on hero slides; tabular numbers everywhere.
- **Holm memo** · confidential pre-read, investor note, law or finance memo · warm cream #E4DFD7, ink, chestnut #52311D accent · serif for the one hero sentence only, sans body, mono caps eyebrows · no boxes, no pure white.
- **Atelier warm paper** · brand story to partners · warm paper, ink, coral dots · tight grotesk + italic serif emphasis · Roman-numeral sections, collage pictures.
- **Cartesian** · white paper, investment thesis, advisory · #EDE8E0 / #E2DBD1, ink, taupe accent #8A8178 · Playfair + Inter · unhurried, low density.
- **Monochrome** · user research, long report, academic · ivory #FAFADF, all-black type · Lora + Jost · no colour at all, ledger feel.
- **Soft Editorial** · longform brand story, gallery, literary · paper #F2EEDF, ink, pink/lemon/blush/sage accents · Cormorant Garamond + Work Sans · low density, pastel chips.
- **Taste editorial** · any calm talk · warm cream, one muted pastel accent pair (sage on mint, brick on blush) · serif display + grotesk body + mono meta · hairline rules, big margins, italic accent inside a title.

## Dark and tech (only for tech subjects)

Taste allows dark grounds and effects only for software, gaming, hardware and fintech subjects; for a cafe or shop deck pick a light look.

- **Tech sharing** · internal tech talk · GitHub-dark, mono, terminal code blocks, agenda + Q&A.
- **Terminal review** · dev-tool review with benchmarks · near-black #0A0C10, mint #7ED3A4 big text, mono throughout · window chrome; scanlines are a note.
- **Tactical telemetry** · ops, monitoring, security talk · charcoal, white phosphor mono, hazard red #E61919 as the one accent · square cells on hairlines, density over decoration.
- **Dark knowledge graph** · dev-tool or data launch · deep night ground, graph overlay on the cover (draw nodes as ellipses and paths) · its blur orbs and gradient text flatten to solid.
- **Dev tutorial dark** · workflow tutorial · GitHub-dark #0D1117, centred layout, pill tags · its purple gradient text becomes one solid accent (not violet on Nomi work).
- **Product launch** · launch keynote · dark hero + light content, warm orange accent, feature cards, pricing tiers.
- **Pitch deck (VC)** · fundraising · white, one accent (its blue-purple gradient becomes solid blue), big numbers, traction chart.
- **8-Bit Orbit** · gaming pitch, hackathon demo · void navy #0A0E27, neon pink, cyan, yellow · Tektur + Chakra Petch · pixel-art arcade.
- **Retro Windows** · retro gaming, Y2K brand, tech-history talk · grey #C0C0C0 chrome, navy title bars · pixel font + system sans.
- **Presenter mode** · talks where the speaker reads a script · dark default · every slide carries a 150-300 word script in its notes.
