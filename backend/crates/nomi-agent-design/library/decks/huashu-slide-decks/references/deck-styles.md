# Twenty slide styles

The Slides section of the style library, numbered for the roulette. Hex values and named fonts are anchors only: derive colour with the protocol in `design/huashu-design-styles` and check fonts against `design/huashu-typography`. Each: lineage, fit, master slides, signature.

## Bold

1. **Neo-Swiss Billboard.** Big-number editorial pitch decks, Businessweek graphics, Pentagram. Fundraising, business reviews, launch key slides. White or near-black ground, one saturated accent, grid lines. Masters: a big colour block with one word; a giant number filling half the slide; split comparison; one full flat chart. Signature: billboard type, strict baseline grid.
2. **Black Big-Number Stage.** Keynote launches, Presentation Zen. Product keynotes, town halls, emotional year reviews. Black ground, white type, one brand accent per slide. Masters: one line centred; a giant number with unit and note; two-column spec comparison; a slogan slide. Lots of negative space.
3. **Mono-Brand Type-as-Hero.** Wrapped-style systems, Collins brand books. Brand and campaign decks. One brand colour flooding the slide, black or white type; type is the picture. Masters: full colour with huge knocked-out type; two colour halves; a number filling the slide.
4. **Full-Bleed Gradient Manifesto.** Story-led sales decks, campaign manifestos. Vision slides, turning points. A full-bleed field with a reversed statement and a hashtag line. In editable PPTX use solid colour instead.
5. **Candy-Color Lecture Stage.** Harvard CS50, the Lessig and Takahashi methods. Lessons, technical talks, code teaching. Near-black ground, one candy-coloured word per slide, rotating colours; highlighted code blocks; very few words.
6. **Playful Maximalist Editorial.** Collins-style brand books. Opinionated brand decks, creative pitches. A loud yellow field, rounded serif headlines, magazine whitespace, playful copy.
7. **Irreverent Pop.** Personality-led ad sales decks, David Carson grid-breaking. Youth brands, meme-friendly pitches, creator decks. Mixed type breaking the grid; "fun" slides then a serious "facts" slide for rhythm.
8. **Maximalist 3D-Type.** Year-in-review cards. Emotional year reviews, shareable vertical cards. Clashing saturated fields, giant inflated numbers, duotone portraits.

## Neutral

9. **Bento Grid.** Keynote bento era, metric card matrices. Feature summaries, data reviews, results. Light or near-black ground, unequal-height cards each with one insight (a number, a line icon, a sparkline), rounded with a hairline.
10. **Dark Hairline Terminal.** Developer-tool decks. Technical launches, engineering reviews. Near-black (never pure), hairline grid, one cool accent, mono labels, extreme whitespace.
11. **Two-Font Consulting.** Consulting brand systems. Reports, executive reviews, research. Deep navy and white with one highlight colour, a characterful serif headline against sans body, a conclusion-style action title top left, magazine split of conclusion text and visual.
12. **Diagram-Driven Isotype.** Enterprise sales decks, Neurath's Isotype, "Say It With Charts". Platforms, customer journeys, methods. Horizontal journey arrows, layered architecture boxes, icon grids, 2 × 2s, waterfalls, pyramids.
13. **Diagrammatic Minimalism.** The Golden Circle talk, Bauhaus geometry. Frameworks, one-idea talks. One geometric master diagram (circles, triangle, matrix) carrying the whole idea, capital labels inside it.
14. **Narrative Sparkline.** Duarte's Resonate, documentary data stories. Change stories, before and after. One waving line across the slide, annotations on the line, the turning point in an accent colour, a curve climbing off the slide.

## Quiet

15. **Assertion-Evidence.** Michael Alley's research, action titles, Tufte, the Minto pyramid. Engineering and policy reviews. A full-sentence title (never a noun phrase), one picture of evidence under it, labels inside the picture, zero bullets.
16. **Institutional Swiss Minimal.** The classic ten-slide seed pitch, Müller-Brockmann. Investor pitches, plain proposals. White, black and grey, one accent; a one-sentence title band; three parallel columns; a layered market-size number; a 2 × 2 competitor matrix.
17. **Editorial Longform.** Annual letters, six-page memos. Year letters, deep internal updates. Off-white ground, prose paragraphs with inline data cards, an occasional huge number as an anchor.
18. **Humanist Rounded Cards.** Friendly learning-platform systems. Education, non-profit, warm brands. Forest green on off-white, serif headlines, large rounded cards, real photography.
19. **Dense Research Report.** Internet trends reports, market maps. Trend reports, data reviews. White, one brand colour stepped while the rest is grey, conclusion-sentence titles, one dense chart per slide, logo market maps, tiny source footnotes.
20. **All-Text Manifesto.** Culture decks, narrative memos. Values, culture, deep memos. White or black, one highlight colour, one assertion per slide, plain candid prose, zero images and bullets.
