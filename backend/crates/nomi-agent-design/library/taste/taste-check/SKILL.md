# Taste: the finish check

The checklist you run on your own canvas before saving each version: first screen, screens and sections, edges, pictures, the right things for the kind, system, contrast, copy, contacts and the phone, each line a thing you can see in the frames.

Go through every screen frame at 1440, 768 and 390. Fix every miss, then check again; say in the handoff anything you left open and why.

## First screen
- [ ] Says what is sold, for whom or where, with one hard fact (price, place, date, number).
- [ ] The hero fills the first screen alone (900 / 1024 / 844) at every width; nothing of the next section shows; content ends by y 836 at 1440.
- [ ] h1 at most three lines, names the category; at most two buttons (a place: one), above the fold at every width.
- [ ] No picture sliced by the fold; at 768 a stacked picture ends whole 24+ above the foot or clearly bleeds.

## Screens and sections
- [ ] Every section frame is exactly 1 or 2 screens tall (photo-chapters 3-4); its h2 first, at the top padding (y 120; 96 on a phone).
- [ ] One idea and one h2 per section; no neighbour's heading or picture in its tile.
- [ ] Content fills 55%+ of its window; no section under 360 of content; short quotes, logo rows or FAQs merged into the section above.
- [ ] Sections separated only by ground change or space; no rules, shadows or inset boxes between them.
- [ ] Neighbours never share a layout; a card layout at most twice; a calm section after at most two visual ones.
- [ ] The close and footer are one section.

## Edges and grid
- [ ] Every block starts on the left content edge (x 80; place 120; phone 24) or a column start, and ends on a column end or the right edge (1360; place 1320; phone 366).
- [ ] Text starts on two lines only: the left edge and the one split line, the same on every split.
- [ ] Splits: text left, visual right, top-aligned, never mirrored; phone: text then visual.
- [ ] Card rows full, one height, first and last on the edges. Nothing staggered, overlapping, crossing a section edge or out to one side.
- [ ] Gaps and paddings from the spacing scale.

## Pictures
- [ ] Every visual is drawn: photo placeholders at final size and ratio with a real tone and a `Photo · subject, framing, light` note; no empty or grey box at any width.
- [ ] No caption, label or credit under any picture; notes are canvas notes only.
- [ ] Photo count fits the kind (place full 12-20, compact 8-12, short under 8); one ratio per row; no photo used twice; none taller than 708 outside the hero.
- [ ] Words on a photo only in the hero or one closing band on tech, product or service, on a named calm area; never on a place.
- [ ] Product: whole in three sections at most, a different view each, every headline promise pictured, every colour shown once.
- [ ] Real brand icons in full colour (or a tile with its note), never a drawn look-alike; interface glyphs from one outline set, one stroke width.

## The right things for the kind
- [ ] Place, product, service: no effect, gradient, glass, grain, loop, counter, dark page or dark hero; a place has no UI pieces, diagrams, feature cards, tabs, logo rows or fact strips.
- [ ] Software shows its product big in one app window; a game names itself in the h1.
- [ ] The hero entrance, one or two moments (with their sections) and the hover layer are annotated; effects (tech only) annotated by name.
- [ ] No carousel, tabs on other kinds, accordion, popup, chat bubble, badge, sticky element besides the nav and phone bar.

## One system
- [ ] One button pair (one filled primary per view, one secondary style), one card style, one radius ladder, one link look, one heading scale, buttons 44 tall (48 on a place).
- [ ] Two boxes deep at most; card text 16px in the ink.
- [ ] Every colour is a `$color-*` token; one accent, under 3% of a screen outside its jobs; nothing violet or lavender.
- [ ] Two font families at most; no Inter, Roboto, Poppins, Montserrat, Plus Jakarta Sans; headlines 400 (500 on tech), never 700.

## Contrast and size
- [ ] Reading text 7:1, muted text 4.5:1, large muted 3:1; every text on a place 4.5:1; text on a brand band 7:1; primary label 4.5:1; accent and focus ring 3:1 on the ground.
- [ ] Nothing under 13px; reading text 16px+; lines at most 62 characters.
- [ ] Every link and control outside running text 44 tall, 8+ from the next target.

## Copy
- [ ] Every heading has a noun; buttons are a verb and an object; no "Pelajari lebih lanjut", "Selengkapnya", "Jelajahi".
- [ ] The main action is worded the same in nav, hero, close and phone bar, and shown at most twice on any screen.
- [ ] No slop word, stock line, virtue word, exclamation mark, all-caps label or em-dash habit; devices within budget (one two-fragment heading, two contrast frames).
- [ ] Nothing invented: no reviews, ratings, quotes, customer counts or logos the brief does not give; assumed hours and prices listed in the handoff.
- [ ] Words per screen within budget (place 44, service 34, product 47, tech 60-80); a page about 900 words.
- [ ] No placeholder, bracket, lorem ipsum, "contoh" or sample note.

## Contacts
- [ ] Address street and district only; no house number, postcode, coordinates, NPWP or register numbers.
- [ ] No invented phone or WhatsApp number; stand-in email only on `.example`, never printed.
- [ ] "Petunjuk arah" only with a street; "Lihat area" with a district only; no map link with only a town.

## Tablet and phone
- [ ] At 768 splits stack text first, panel cards take the full row, no row half empty, each section still 1 or 2 screens.
- [ ] At 390 one column (or 165 two-up tiles), buttons full width, the menu a full-screen sheet; a place has the phone action bar.
- [ ] Nothing past 390 sideways, nothing on top of anything else.

## Your eyes
- [ ] Beside figma.com and x.ai it reads as a professional company's site, made for this client.
- [ ] The hero is the work itself, not a mood; the page shows the real thing on every screen.
