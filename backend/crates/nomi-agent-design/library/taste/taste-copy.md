# Taste: copy and voice

How to write every word on the canvas: the five rules, lengths and words per screen, what may be assumed, contacts, the voice and its registers, the moves for each part, and the slop words and tells to lint for, with notes for Bahasa Indonesia.

Write all copy before drawing, in the person's language (often Bahasa Indonesia). Every sentence is one the owner would say out loud to a customer: what it is and what it does, understood by a stranger in five seconds. No metaphors, invented terms or ad wording.

## The five rules

1. **The h1 names the category, and for a brand nobody knows, the buyer or the place,** in 2 to 8 words. A place: what and where in 40 characters at most ("Toko roti di Jl. Malioboro"; "Penginapan enam kamar di Ubud"). A game: its name and genre. Never a riddle, command, mood, or brand line.
2. **The first screen holds one hard fact:** a number, a place, a price or a date ("mulai Rp 249.000", "buka 07.00").
3. **Every heading has a noun that names a thing:** the product, a part, the place, a section noun (harga, kamar, menu, jam buka, pertanyaan, lokasi) or a digit. One brand line (the page's one figure of speech) a page at most, never the h1, with a fact under it.
4. **Buttons are a verb plus what the visitor gets,** 1 to 5 words: Pesan kue, Booking kamar, Mulai coba gratis, Lihat harga, Unduh katalog, Kirim permintaan. A bare noun only where the link opens that thing. Never "Pelajari lebih lanjut", "Selengkapnya", "Jelajahi", "Temukan", "Klik di sini" (Learn more, Read more, Discover, Explore, More, Click here).
5. **Few actions, one wording each:** at most 2 in the hero (one on a place), 10 different labels a page. The main action names the transaction and reads word for word the same in the nav, the hero, the close and the phone bar, and nowhere else. One screen shows it twice at most.

## Lengths

- **Hero sub-line:** software, product, service: how it works and the main benefit, 15 to 30 words. A place: the fact with today's hours, 80 characters max ("Roti sourdough dan kue lapis dari 07.00, Selasa sampai Minggu.").
- **Section headings:** 1 to 6 words, two lines max: a noun phrase ("Kamar dan harga"), a verb with its thing ("Kirim invoice dari HP"), or a plain claim ("Gaji karyawan cair di hari yang sama"). Never a bare command, a question (FAQ entries aside), a colon heading, or a line that only makes sense after reading the page.
- **A heading's grey half** is its own sentence after a full stop: "Kamar dan harga. *Sarapan sudah termasuk.*"
- **Under a heading:** one line to two sentences, 10 to 40 words. Card body 1-2 sentences, max 25 words, one concrete detail. Card titles: a verb and an object, one line, never a coy label.
- **Words per screen:** product 47, place 44, service 34; a page about 900 words at most, footer aside. Tech (software, gaming, hardware): 60 to 80 words a screen outside UI mocks; cut in this order: a second sentence under a heading, card bodies to one sentence of 12-18 words, a fourth card, a list a picture already shows.
- **Numbers** in sentences with their unit, uneven where real ("4,5 jam seminggu", 212, 38%). A number never breaks from its unit across a line.
- Paragraphs at most five lines at 1440; lines at most 62 characters.

## What may be assumed (nothing else is invented)

- Prices, address, hours and dates come from the brief. Only two things may be assumed: a coined brand name (not a dictionary word, not a real business), and on a place plausible hours and prices for the trade, uneven and local (Rp 28.000, not Rp 30.000). Mark each assumed value in your handoff.
- A product or service with no price says "Harga sesuai permintaan" or shows none.
- **No invented proof:** no reviews, ratings, quotes, testimonials, statistics, customer counts, customer names, portraits, client logos, awards or press lines the brief does not give. Without proof: a strip of plain facts from the brief, or nothing.
- **No placeholders or sample notes:** no brackets, lorem ipsum, "contoh", "(sample)", "segera hadir" unless the brief says so. Write the real words or leave the element out.
- **No caption under any visual.** A heading names the set; a sold item's name and price sit inside its card.
- **Credits:** one short footer line naming only the photo sources; with real logos also "Nama produk dan logo adalah merek dagang pemiliknya".
- **Each fact once,** one value, one wording, repeated only where it belongs (hero, visit, close, footer).

## Contacts

- **Address:** street and district only: "Jl. Malioboro, Gedongtengen". No house number, RT/RW, postcode or coordinates. No street in the brief: the district alone (and "Lihat area" instead of "Petunjuk arah"); only a town: no map link at all.
- **Phone and WhatsApp:** none unless the brief gives one; never invent a number. A given number is printed as given. A "Chat lewat WhatsApp" button is fine when the brief gives the number.
- **Email:** a form, or a button ("Kirim email") whose address is on `.example` (halo@namabisnis.example) as a stand-in Koda swaps. Never print an email address, domain or social handle; no social icons unless the brief gives the accounts.
- **No company details:** no NPWP, NIB, register or tax numbers. Legal row: brand, current year, Privasi, Ketentuan, credits line.
- **Person names** only from the brief. In mock UI, names fit the town and generation (Siti Rahayu, Budi Santoso), never the model default cast (Priya, Okafor, Marcus, Elara...).
- **Coined names** never the model favourites: Kestrel, Halcyon, Lumen, Nimbus, Fieldnote, Brightloop, Acme, Northwind, Apex, Zenith, Vertex.

## Voice

- **The model's default voice is out:** warm, wry, modest, a little literary, ending paragraphs on a deadpan twist, calling itself honest or calm. Copy the client's own voice first (their old site, captions, the brief's wording).
- **The fix is facts and decisions, never synonyms.** Each detail is true, each device used once, the page uneven where the material is.
- Pick one register by the buyer, price and real character; write a one-line voice card in your plan (register, person "Anda"/"kamu" or "kami", sentence length, where humour may sit, house words, never-list).

| Register: how it sounds | Fits |
|---|---|
| Facts only: names, dates, counts | firms, clinics, B2B |
| Technical, dry: label plus hard figure, verb-led steps | developer tools, AI |
| Founder's letter: first person, a named enemy habit | small software, founder-led |
| Playful: straight face, fact under each joke | snacks, drinks, playful apps |
| Literary: exact words, materials by name | skincare, design objects |
| Restraint: places, buildings, history | heritage hotels, architects |
| Sensory, "you": what you see and carry, literally | cafes, food, spas, gyms |
| Historical scene: opens on a dated moment | family restaurants, old firms |
| Activist: one measurable mission | mission brands, non-profits |
| Refusal: what it will not do | makers, repair shops |
| Product as hero: the product is the subject | hardware, one-feature apps |
| Hurried, conversational: asides in brackets | freelancers, small studios |

Voice holds, tone moves: prices, FAQ, booking, checkout and forms go plain; errors and confirmations short and kind.

## Moves for each part

- **Hero forms that name the category:** category and buyer ("Aplikasi kasir untuk warung kopi"), category and place, category and a true unexpected promise, category and scope, category, place and year (only from the brief), a narrow superlative the brief proves.
- **Features:** the mechanism in verb-led steps; the outcome first, the feature as proof; real values (times, sizes, amounts) in examples.
- **About:** start in a real moment from the brief, end in a modest present tense ("Sekarang kami bersebelas, ovennya masih sama"); values as behaviours with numbers. No story section without a real story.
- **Prices, FAQ, booking:** facts only; perks priced literally; a risk reversal beside the button only when true ("Batal gratis sampai H-2"); the FAQ admits a limit.
- **Close:** restate the hero's promise with one fact and the main action word for word.
- **Microcopy carries the voice:** form errors name the field and what to type ("Tulis alamat email dengan @"), the sent line names what was sent in the button's words ("Pesanan kue Anda sudah kami terima. Kami balas sebelum jam 12 besok."), empty states, 404.
- **Footer:** facts only, no sign-off line, no pun on the name.

## Slop and tells (lint your own copy)

- **Banned words** (each stands in for a missing fact): delve, tapestry, realm, embark, beacon, multifaceted, paradigm, myriad, plethora, meticulous, intricate, elevate, unlock, unleash, empower, harness, supercharge, seamless, effortless, cutting-edge, revolutionary, transformative, holistic, next-level, next-generation, world-class, best-in-class, game-changing, state-of-the-art, bespoke, synergy, leverage, reimagine, powerful, robust, nestled, unparalleled. A word literal for the trade stays (a seamless knit).
- **Indonesian equivalents to treat the same way** (Sora's translation of the same tells): solusi terbaik, inovatif, revolusioner, terdepan, berkelas dunia, kualitas terbaik, terpercaya, profesional dan berpengalaman, mudah dan cepat, praktis dan efisien, satu-satunya (unproven), serba ada / one-stop solution, tingkatkan bisnis Anda, wujudkan impian Anda, hadir untuk Anda.
- **Stock lines:** "Di era digital seperti sekarang", "Ucapkan selamat tinggal pada", "Bergabunglah dengan ribuan", "Dipercaya oleh", "Bayangkan dunia di mana", "Siap mengubah X Anda?", "X jadi mudah", "di ujung jari Anda", "hanya dengan beberapa klik", "Bukan sekadar X, tapi Y".
- **Virtue words about ourselves** (ban): jujur, tenang, sungguh, benar-benar, cukup (simply), tulus, penuh perhatian, berdedikasi; honest, calm, quiet, actually, genuinely, truly, simply, carefully.
- **Dead metaphors:** ekosistem, perjalanan (unless travel), "di jantung kota", "di mana X bertemu Y", landscape, hub, journey.
- **Device budget per page:** two-fragment heading "X. Y." 1; number-pair heading 0-1; negation run "Tanpa X. Tanpa Y." 1; contrast frames ("bukan X, tapi Y", "alih-alih", "tanpa ribet") 2 in all; dry closing lines 2, never in a row; eyebrow labels 1-2; step numbers only where order is the content; testimonials only from the brief; "Sejak [tahun]" once, from the brief.
- **Second-generation tells:** invented specifics (batch numbers, a minute of the morning), a strip of three invented figures under the hero, polished three-quote testimonials, the origin template, the same section skeleton every time, "Paling populer" on the middle plan by reflex, process talk ("Di bawah ini Anda akan menemukan"), a stale copyright year, perfect evenness.
- **Punctuation and case:** no exclamation marks, no em dashes as a habit, no emoji as bullets, no all-caps or tracked-caps labels (sentence case everywhere, labels 13/500), curly quotes, italic only for quotes and contact lines.

## Indonesian notes

- Rupiah with a dot for thousands and a space: "Rp 249.000"; "mulai Rp 95.000" for a from-price; never "Rp249.000,-" or "IDR". Dates "26 Jul 2026"; times with a full stop, "07.00-16.00"; day ranges "Selasa-Minggu".
- Choose "Anda" (formal, most services, clinics, B2B) or "kamu" (cafes, youth brands, playful apps) once per page and keep it.
- Sentence case, not Title Case, even though local sites often capitalise every word.
- Indonesian lines run longer than English; when a heading wraps past its limit, cut words, never shrink the type.
- Avoid English filler mixed in ("one-stop", "best quality", "trusted") unless it is the client's own wording.

## Check before drawing

Read each line aloud as the owner. Put a rival's name into the h1 and each heading: a line that still reads true says nothing about this client; rewrite it from the fact sheet.
