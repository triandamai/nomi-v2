# Taste: the section library

Which sections each page kind uses and in what order, and how to compose each one as frames on the grid: navs, features, photographs, proof, prices, place sections, closes and footers.

Every section is one frame, 900 or 1800 tall at 1440, its h2 first, one idea, its picture taking a real share (half a tile or more, 50/50 in a split, a whole screen as a layer). Numbers below are at 1440 unless marked; phone stacks text first, then the picture, 342 wide. Splits are the page's one split, text left.

## Page plans by kind (order on the page; [optional])

- **Software** (and AI, fintech, developer tools): nav, hero, [logo row docked in the hero], **app-frame** as section 2 or 3, two or three feature sections in different layouts (features-2x2, bento, split-text-left, tabs-tour, chapter, steps), [integrations as a hub diagram card; a grid only for 9+ tools], [quote with a stat, or customer proof, only from the brief], [pricing-three, pricing-two or comparison-table], faq, closing. Nav `mega|cards|centre`, footer `dark|statement|columns`, close `type|word`, rhythm `dark-band|surface-pairs|space`, buttons `solid|pill|tonal|arrow`.
- **Gaming:** nav, hero (the h1 is the game's name and genre, the launch date or status the brief gives), [trailer frame], the world (one or two full-bleed sections), what you do (three to five open cards, one verb each over a capture), [a game system as a diagram], platforms and editions (real Steam, PlayStation, Epic marks; Xbox and Nintendo named in type), [press or quote from the brief], [faq], closing. No UI fragments, dashboards or widgets. Nav `mega|centre|cards`, footer `dark|statement`, close `word|type`, rhythm `dark-band|media-band|space`, buttons `solid|pill|block`.
- **Product:** nav, hero, the range (line-up) within the first three sections, two or three feature sections (one benefit each, each its own composition), specs (ruled rows or a comparison table), proof from the brief, [faq, five questions max], closing with newsletter. Two or three photos a screen; the product whole in three sections at most, a different picture each; every headline promise pictured; every colour offered shown once; no fact twice. Prices "from", never monthly plans. Nav `cards|split|mega`, footer `columns|dark`, close `photo|type|word`, rhythm `dark-band|brand-band`, buttons `arrow|solid|block|pill`.
- **Service:** nav, hero, services-list, work (3-6 project photos, or a photo-pair), steps, people-row (only the real team), proof from the brief, [pricing-two or a ruled price list], [faq], booking-form with the address, closing. Nav `mega|split|centre`, footer `visit|dark|columns`, close `type|photo`, rhythm `surface-pairs|dark-band|space`, buttons `solid|tonal|block|arrow`.
- **Place:** see Place below.

Mark (when the client has no logo): a wordmark, an initial tile or a simple shape (arc, ring, stack, dots) beside the name at 20px.

## Navs (72 tall, one opaque bar)

- **nav-mega:** logo, 4-6 words, primary right. A word opens a full-width flat panel: two or three link columns on span-3 (a 13px label over 3-4 links, each a name plus one grey line of at most 30 characters) and one picture card on span-3 ending on the right edge.
- **nav-cards:** the panel holds three span-4 picture cards (a panel 200 tall with the product's screen or photo, the title, one grey line up to 52 characters). Place: three 384x288 photo cards with name and price from.
- **nav-centre:** words centred on the page axis; logo and primary on the edges. Four or five words.
- **nav-split** (places, studios): three or four words in the left third, the logo and name (28px, display face) on the page centre, the primary in the right third.

## Features and product

- **app-frame** (every software page, hero or first three sections): h2 on span-5 and a grey paragraph (~25 words) with one link on span-6 from x 732; 48 below, one app window across the content (about 1280 x 550, one radius 12-20, 1px border) with the list pane (5/12: 9+ rows, person, item and detail, a status chip) beside the open item (7/12: the product's real subject: a diff for code review, an invoice, a conversation, a calendar). Both panes hold more rows than fit, cut by the window's bottom edge (a clipping frame; Taste's fade cannot be drawn, so the cut falls between rows, never through text). Phone: the open item alone.
- **features-2x2:** h2 with a grey run-on; four span-6 cards, each a title (verb and object), two lines of 16-20 words in ink, then a panel 336 tall holding one real UI piece cut by the panel's bottom or right edge. Four different pieces. Product or service: a photo fills each panel.
- **bento:** row one span-8 + span-4, row two three span-4; one panel height for the group (336). Wide card: the main feature; small ones 2-4 word titles.
- **split-text-left:** text on span-5 (h2; 24; two or three sentences at 20px; up to three short facts; one link) and a 5:4 frame on span-6 from x 732 holding the UI cut by the frame's bottom, or (product, service) a 628x471 photo of exactly what the text names.
- **tabs-tour** (software only, click only): span-5 h2 and 3-6 tabs (verb-and-object labels), a fixed-size frame on span-6. Draw the first tab active.
- **chapter:** h2 span-5, grey paragraph span-6; 64 below a wide 16:7 frame of the area's main screen; 48 below four span-3 feature names with one grey line each. A series of two to four may share the layout.
- **steps:** only with three real steps: three span-4 cards, a small step number, title, one or two lines, a 336 panel (service: a photo). No arrows between cards, no huge numerals.
- **product-statement** (products): an h2 statement on span-8 (first sentence ink, the rest muted), a 2:1 frame across the content with the product photo, then three facts on span-4 (1px rule, value 28px with unit, grey label).
- **line-up:** h2 ("Tiga ukuran") with a link on its row; three span-4 (or four span-3) tiles: the product from one angle on one backdrop, then name, "mulai Rp 249.000", one facts line.
- **photo-band** (product, service; never a place): one full-bleed photo layer section, an h2 of at most eight words on its calm dark part. Once a page.
- **photo-pair:** h2 and one line, then two span-6 photos, one ratio, one light, nothing under them.
- **services-list:** h2, one line and ruled rows on span-5 (name, one line, "mulai Rp 95.000"), a 4:5 or 4:3 photo on span-6. Max eight rows. Never icon cards.
- **peek-list:** like services-list, but each row has its own photo; draw the first row active with its photo shown.
- **people-row:** four span-3 tiles, a 4:5 portrait placeholder, name, role, one line. Only people the brief names.
- **integrations:** a hub diagram card (the product in the centre, real tool icons on spokes) inside a feature section; the grid (eight 72px tiles across) only for 9+ tools the brief lists.
- **client slots** (trailer, key art, screenshots, CAD): a frame at the file's final ratio holding a placeholder that reads finished, its note naming the client's file.

## Proof (only from the brief; none is a normal page)

- **logo-row:** docked under the hero, up to six real customer logos 28 tall, one 14px grey line above. Software and product only.
- **quote-stat:** merged into the section above: quote span-8 (28px, max 40 words), a 40px face and name, role, town under it; a stat span-3 from x 1058 (38px value, grey line up to 8 words).
- **customer-proof-row:** three span-4 cards: logo or name, story title, one line, a link, a stat panel. Customers the brief names only.

## Prices and questions

- **pricing-three** (software): three span-4 plan cards, the middle one marked recommended; name, who it is for, price with "/bulan", one full-width button (filled only on the recommended), 5-7 lines.
- **pricing-two:** span-4 h2 with what every plan includes, two span-4 plan cards.
- **comparison-table:** rows on the grid, 48 tall, each closed by a 1px rule; the feature on span-4, options on span-4 or span-2; checks, grey dashes, short values. Phone: each option its own block, never sideways.
- **faq:** the split: h2 "Pertanyaan", one grey line and a link left; 5-8 questions right, each answer open (no accordion), 1px rule under each. Questions max 60 characters, answers under 50 words starting with the answer.
- **booking-form:** the split: h2, what happens next, then the form on `$color-muted` with 48 padding: at most six fields, the primary, a status line and the reply line (`taste-app-screens` for field rules).

## Place (cafe, warung, bakery, guesthouse, salon, shop, local studio)

Photographs, type and flat colour carry it. Never on a place: effects, gradients, UI fragments, diagrams, feature or plan cards, tabs, accordions, logo rows, fact strips, timelines, carousels, words on a photo, rounded photos, a dark section other than one brand band.

- **Length by photo count:** full page 12-20 photos, at most 6 screens (hero, visit, the offer, [second offer], the place, orders or booking, closing); compact 8-12 photos, 4-5 screens (hero, visit, offer, [place], closing); short page under 8 photos: 3 screens (hero, one section holding the offer and the visit, closing).
- **Visit is always screen 2:** a span-6 panel on `$color-muted` (48 padding) left, a 1:1 or 4:3 photo of the shopfront or the room from the door right. Panel: h2 "Kunjungi kami" (or the street set large above, see type moment), the address on two lines at 20/30, the week's hours (days left, times right, one line per day or range), one secondary button "Petunjuk arah" only when the main action is something else, at most two 44px rows (parking, step-free access, delivery area).
- **The offer:** a head row (h2 left, 40 characters max; on its baseline at the right one 16px line and a link "Menu lengkap"), 56 to the content. Menu or prices as text groups: a 13px label, 12 of air, ruled rows 44 tall (64 with a description), name 16/500 left, price right in tabular figures ("Rp 28.000"), description 16 muted; max 6 rows with descriptions or 8 without per group; groups hold the same number of rows. Rooms: 4:3 room photo, name 20/28, facts 16 muted ("2 tamu · 18 m² · sisi taman"), price from with season, "Booking kamar ini" link. Goods: four 1:1 linked cards on span-3 (282), name and price inside the card, flat fill one step off the ground or a hairline, no radius.
- **Compositions** (the offer and the place each a different one, neighbours never the same; square corners, one grade):
  - `bleed`: head row, then one photo across the whole viewport, 1440x560 (phone 390x488). Once, never right after the hero.
  - `pair`: two span-6 photos, same ratio (588x441 4:3, or 588x735 4:5 in a two-screen section), tops level.
  - `trio`: three photos on span-5 (486x607, 4:5), span-4 (384x384, 1:1), span-3 (282x212, 4:3), tops on one line, one subject family.
  - `strip`: four 1:1 details on span-3 (282x282) or six on span-2 (180x180), hands, tools, materials.
  - `tall-small`: a 4:5 on span-6 (588x735) left; on span-5 right the h2 and lines at top and a 1:1 on span-3 (282x282) at the bottom, feet level.
  - `wall`: photos from edge to edge with 0 or 2 gap. `story`: text left (h2, 3-6 lines at 20/30 on who runs it and since when, one link), a 4:3 photo of people at work right (588x441). One story a page.
- **One type moment:** the address (street and district, e.g. "Jl. Malioboro, Gedongtengen") set large under the visit h2 (48-104px, display face, tracking -3%), or the business name set across the content in the close. Ink on the ground, never outline or on a photo.
- **Orders:** three span-4 kinds, each a 4:3 photo, 13px label, title 20/28, one or two sentences with lead time and price from, one text link.
- **Booking:** head row ("Booking kamar"; "Kami balas dalam sehari."), a 4:3 photo on span-6 beside the form on `$color-muted`, equal heights.
- **Close:** h2 (max eight words), one line ("Buka hari ini 07.00-16.00"), the main action, a 1:1 photo not used above.
- **Footer-visit:** address 32/40 on span-7 with one line on how to get there, the hours from x 732, the nav row, the legal row; then the name set wide (max 12vw, about 170px).
- **Trade orders** (after hero and visit): cafe: menu, the place, beans if sold. Bakery: goods with prices, orders, the process. Guesthouse: rooms, the house, booking (hero: its region's landscape). Shop: new in (goods), categories as a text list, one story. Workshop/bengkel: service prices as ruled rows with "Booking servis", story.

## Closes and footers (one section: `closing`)

- **close-type:** h2 (max eight words), an optional muted line (trial terms, hours), one big primary button: across the content, 88 tall, 32px label (software); 640 wide, 72 tall (product, service). Phone: full width, 48 tall.
- **close-photo:** head row (h2, one line, the main action), then a photo not used above from edge to edge down to the links. Place: a 1:1 on span-5 beside the words.
- **close-word:** h2 and button on one row, then the business name set across the content in the display face.
- **footer-columns:** company block span-4 (logo, one line at 28px), four span-2 columns named like the nav, the legal row (brand, year, Privasi, Ketentuan, the credits line). **footer-dark:** the same on the inverse ground, name at 48px. **footer-visit:** as on a place. **footer-statement** (tech): close and columns on the lit ground, inset 8 with large corners.
