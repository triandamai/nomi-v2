# Brands: enterprise, telecom and super-apps

Enterprise, telecom and super-app systems: quiet frames, one owned colour, application chrome, and what a professional service can learn.

**Learn, don't copy.** These cards teach how a system works. Never reproduce a brand's identity for another business, and never name the brand in the person's design. Take structure and character, then set the person's own colours and type.

Token names are Sora's `$color-<name>`; the number after a colour is its contrast with `background`, and after primary-foreground its contrast with primary. A value marked * means the accent is under 3:1 on the ground, so buttons and links take the ink and the accent becomes `$color-brand` (Taste's * rule).

**Tokens:** background #ededed · foreground #1a1a1a (15) · card #f7f7f7 · muted #f7f7f7 · muted-foreground #6a6a6a (4.6) · border #e0e0e0 · primary #128742 (3.9 on ground) · primary-foreground #ffffff (4.6) · destructive #ce2430 (4.6) · radius 4/8/16 · type 15/16/18/24/32/48 · body line 1.6 · display tracking -0.01em
**Fixed:** muted-foreground #888888 → #6a6a6a (3.0:1); primary #07c160 → #128742 so its label #ffffff holds 4.5 (2.4:1); destructive #fa5151 → #ce2430 (2.8:1).
**Fonts:** PingFang SC (system) → Rethink Sans
**Learn:** A booking or order flow that lives inside a chat app: flat white lists on grey, one green action, short labels.
