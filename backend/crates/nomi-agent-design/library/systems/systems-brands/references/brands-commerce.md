# Brands: commerce and consumer

Retail, hospitality and consumer-app systems: what makes each work, its tokens in Sora's names, and what a shop, cafe or homestay can learn.

**Learn, don't copy.** These cards teach how a system works. Never reproduce a brand's identity for another business, and never name the brand in the person's design. Take structure and character, then set the person's own colours and type.

Token names are Sora's `$color-<name>`; the number after a colour is its contrast with `background`, and after primary-foreground its contrast with primary. A value marked * means the accent is under 3:1 on the ground, so buttons and links take the ink and the accent becomes `$color-brand` (Taste's * rule).

## Airbnb
`airbnb` · hotel, retail, wedding · VoltAgent awesome-design-md via open-design

**Works:** The interface vanishes behind photos: white ground, #222222 ink, soft 8/14/20 radii, and the coral spent only on search, the active tab and the main action. Cards are photo-first with three short text lines.
**Tokens:** background #ffffff · foreground #222222 (16) · card #ffffff · muted #f7f7f7 · muted-foreground #6a6a6a (5.4) · border #dddddd · primary #e7164a (4.6 on ground) · primary-foreground #ffffff (4.6) · destructive #c13515 (5.5) · radius 8/14/20 · type 16/20/22/28/44/56 · body line 1.43 · display tracking -0.02em
**Fixed:** primary #ff385c → #e7164a so its label #ffffff holds 4.5 (3.5:1).
**Fonts:** Cereal (licensed) → Figtree for both
**Learn:** A homestay or villa page: let room photos carry the colour, keep one accent for "Pesan", and give every listing card the same photo ratio and three lines.
