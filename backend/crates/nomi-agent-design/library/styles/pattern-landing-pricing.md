# Pattern: Landing and Pricing Content

What a landing page and a pricing section must answer before they look good — one offer, one audience, one action; proof beside the claim; plans that reduce doubt — as a content checklist you fill before drawing; section order and layouts come from taste-sections, copy rules from taste-copy.

## Landing page: fill this first

A landing page wins one intent: **one offer → one audience → one primary action.** A homepage serves many; do not mix them. Before drawing, write down:

| Question | Example |
|---|---|
| The one action | "Pesan katering" (trial, booking, order, WhatsApp chat, download) |
| The offer, exactly | Nasi kotak untuk 20–200 orang, antar se-Yogyakarta |
| Who, and their problem | Panitia acara kantor yang butuh makanan tepat waktu |
| Top 3 objections | Telat datang? Porsi kecil? Minimal pesan berapa? |
| Where visitors come from | Instagram ads, Google Maps, WhatsApp broadcast |
| Proof the person has | Real reviews, photos, client names, numbers they can stand behind |
| Risk reversal | Free tasting, DP 30%, refund if late, free cancellation until H-3 |

If the action or the offer is unknown, ask; everything else can be assumed and marked.

### Above the fold, every time

1. Headline: outcome + audience ("Nasi kotak untuk acara kantor, datang sebelum jam makan").
2. One supporting line: how, and for whom.
3. The primary button with a specific verb.
4. One proof signal, real: a review line, a client name, a count the person gave you.
5. The hero visual: the product, the place, or the app doing the job.

Then argument (problem → how it works in 3 steps → benefits as outcome + detail → proof next to the claim it supports), then objections (FAQ, risk reversal), then the same action again.

- Match the source: if traffic comes from an ad, the hero repeats the ad's promise and tone.
- One primary button per viewport. Secondary actions are links or outline buttons.
- Specific beats general: "Rekap harian dari 40 menit jadi 5 menit", not "Hemat waktu Anda".
- High-friction offers (large orders, contracts) move the FAQ higher.

## Pricing: reduce uncertainty

Your job is not to show prices; it is to help the visitor choose and feel fine about it.

### Gather

- The unit of value: per bulan, per sesi, per orang, per kg, per malam, per paket.
- Plans with real prices, and the 3–6 limits that actually differ.
- The upgrade trigger: what makes someone move up ("lebih dari 3 kasir", "lebih dari 100 tamu").
- Risk reducer: free trial, free plan, money-back, "tanya dulu" with a response time.

### Choose a shape

| Shape | When | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Ruled price list | Services and menus (salon, bengkel, laundry, warung) | Name · note · duration/unit · price per row; groups by category; no tiers |
| Two plans | Simple product, or two audiences (Perorangan / Tim) | Split by who first, then price |
| Three plans | Software or packages with natural tiers | One marked recommended, for a stated reason |
| Usage-based | Price scales with seats, orders, guests | Obvious math, a sensible default ("50 tamu") |
| Custom last mile | Self-serve plus a sales path | The custom card promises a response time, not mystery |

### Rules

- Three plans maximum unless there is a strong reason.
- Plan description is two lines: who it is for, what it unlocks ("Usaha tumbuh — untuk 2–5 kasir, laporan stok harian").
- Bullets are outcomes, not internal feature names: "Kirim invoice dari HP" beats "Modul invoice".
- Every price has its unit and period: "Rp 249.000 / bulan". No hidden fees; "mulai dari" only if pricing truly varies.
- Monthly/yearly toggle only if yearly exists; show the real saving ("Hemat Rp 498.000 per tahun").
- The recommended plan differs in content, not only colour; one badge, no glow.
- Same button verb on every plan ("Mulai coba gratis" on all, or "Pilih paket" on all); filled only on the recommended.
- On the phone frame, plans stack as cards in order of likely choice; never a sideways-scrolling table.
- A comparison table, if needed, groups rows (Dasar, Tim, Laporan, Bantuan) and highlights what changes.

### FAQ that earns its place

Answer the objections people actually raise: Bisa berhenti kapan saja? Apa yang terjadi kalau melewati batas? Ada diskon untuk tahunan atau koperasi? Cara bayar (transfer, QRIS, e-wallet)? Refund? Data saya aman di mana? 6–10 questions, plain question-and-answer, answers in two sentences.

## Never

- Invented proof, counts or ratings near a price.
- "Hubungi kami" in place of every price when prices exist.
- Changing the unit of value halfway down the page.
- Several competing buttons above the fold.
