# Domain name ideas

Suggest web addresses a customer can hear once, say back and type correctly, and tell the person honestly what still needs checking.

## Step 1: understand the business

Note what is sold, who buys, where, and the words customers already use for it ("Roti Bu Sri", "laundry kiloan Condongcatur"). The shop's existing name usually beats any invention.

## Step 2: generate options in groups

1. **The name as it is:** rotibusri.
2. **Name plus trade:** busri-bakery style without the hyphen: busribakery.
3. **Name plus place:** rotibusrijogja.
4. **A short action or coined word,** only when there is no existing name. Coined names follow `taste-copy` (not a dictionary word, not a real business, never the model favourites like Lumen, Nimbus, Kestrel).

Aim for 8 to 12 options, then cut to the best 5.

## What makes a good address

- **Short:** under 15 characters before the extension.
- **Sayable:** someone hears it in a voice note and types it right. Avoid letters that sound alike in Indonesian spelling (f/v, c/k, double letters like "kk").
- **Spellable:** no numbers, no hyphens.
- **Descriptive or memorable:** hints at the trade, or is the brand everyone already knows.
- **Lasts:** still makes sense if they open a second branch or add products (avoid "rotimanis" if they'll sell savoury too).
- **Matches social handles:** the same name free on Instagram and TikTok is a real advantage.

## Extensions

| Extension | Fits | Note |
|---|---|---|
| .com | anyone; most trusted internationally | often taken |
| .id | Indonesian business or person | short, local, needs Indonesian ID |
| .co.id | registered Indonesian company | requires company documents |
| .my.id / .biz.id | individuals, small shops, first sites | cheap, local |
| .web.id | personal or community sites | cheap |
| .store / .shop | online shops | clearly a shop |
| .app / .dev / .io / .ai | apps, developer tools, AI products | tech audience |

## Step 3: present honestly

- You can't check availability or prices yourself. Say so, list the options, and suggest they search a registrar (PANDI-accredited for .id) before deciding. Never write "tersedia!" for a name you haven't checked.
- Give a **top pick and a runner-up** with one line each on why.
- Next steps: check availability, check the Instagram and TikTok handles, say it aloud to two people, check that no other business in the same trade uses it (a trademark search at DJKI for a brand they'll invest in).

> Pilihan utama: **rotibusri.id** — sama dengan nama toko, pendek, mudah diucapkan.
> Cadangan: **rotibusrijogja.com** — kalau .id sudah dipakai.
> Belum saya cek ketersediaannya; cari di situs registrar sebelum membayar.

## On the canvas

Never print a real-looking domain or email on the page: contacts use `.example` stand-ins (halo@rotibusri.example) until the person confirms the real address (`taste-copy`). The chosen name can appear in the nav logo text and footer only once it's decided.
