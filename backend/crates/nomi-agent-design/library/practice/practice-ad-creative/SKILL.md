# Ad creative

Write ads from what real customers say, fit every line to the platform's limits, and draw static ads as calm frames where one sentence or one photo does the work.

Ads matter to you because they are the door to the site you design: the ad's promise and the landing section's first screen must say the same thing in the same words. Video production and image generators are out of scope; you write copy and draw static frames.

## Before writing, gather

1. **Platform and format:** Instagram/Facebook feed or story, TikTok, Google search, marketplace banner.
2. **What is promoted:** the product, an offer, a booking, a free trial. The one action.
3. **Who and how aware:** do they know the problem, the kind of solution, this business?
4. **Real material:** the person's best past posts, customer reviews (Google Maps, Shopee, Tokopedia), WhatsApp questions, comments under past posts. Comments hold objections ("bisa kirim ke Bekasi?") and unprompted praise, both great angles.
5. **Constraints:** words the owner never uses, regulated claims (health, halal, finance, beauty before/after).

**Grounding rule (it agrees with Taste):** every ad traces to a source: a review, a past post, a comment, a brief fact. No invented claims, numbers, testimonials or customer names, ever. If there is no material at all, ask for it instead of writing plausible-sounding ads.

## Step 1: three to five angles

An angle is a reason to click. Each taps a different motive:

| Angle | Example for a laundry in Condongcatur |
|---|---|
| Pain | "Cucian numpuk, waktu habis." |
| Outcome | "Baju bersih dan terlipat besok pagi." |
| Identity | "Untuk anak kos di sekitar UGM." |
| Comparison (honest, no names) | "Ditimbang di depan Anda." |
| Specific fact | "Rp 7.000 per kilo, antar-jemput gratis 3 km." |

Taste wins on two classic angles: **social proof** only with the person's real figures ("Join 10.000+" is out without them), and **urgency** only when true (a real closing date or real limited slots).

## Step 2: match the awareness stage

| Reader | Needs | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Doesn't know the problem | to recognise a moment | a feeling or a short exchange: "Besok kondangan, kemeja belum disetrika." |
| Knows the problem | a credible way out | pain plus outcome, under ten words: "Setrika numpuk? Ambil besok jam 8." |
| Knows solutions | why this one | a state change or one documented number |
| Knows this business | an objection answered | proof, a price, a guarantee: "Luntur? Kami ganti." (only if the policy is real) |

When unsure, state your stage assumption and offer two versions for two stages. A dialogue line that isn't a real customer's words is labelled as a dramatisation, never presented as a testimonial.

## Step 3: variations and limits

Vary angle first, then wording, specificity (number versus general), tone and length. Ten variations that open the same way read as generated.

| Platform | Field | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Google search | headline | 30 characters, up to 15; each must make sense alone and in any combination |
| | description | 90 characters, up to 4 |
| | path | 15 characters, 2 |
| Meta (Facebook, Instagram) | primary text | 125 visible (up to 2,200): put the hook first |
| | headline | about 40 |
| | description | about 30 |
| TikTok | ad text | 80 recommended, 100 max |
| LinkedIn | intro / headline | 150 / 70 recommended |
| X | post / card headline | 280 / 70 |

Show the character count after each line and trim anything over. Google needs at least one keyword headline, one benefit headline and two or three action headlines.

## Writing standards

- Specific beats vague ("Siap dalam 24 jam" over "Cepat"); benefits over features; active voice.
- Use the exact business and product names the brief gives, every time.
- No "terbaik", "nomor satu", "terpercaya" without proof; no all caps, no "!!!".
- Never promise what the landing page can't deliver.
- **Cut AI tells:** "Bukan X, tapi Y" reveals; lists of "Tanpa X, tanpa Y, tanpa Z" (keep the one that matters); "Hasilnya? ..." self-answered questions; stock phrases ("Ucapkan selamat tinggal pada", "Bawa ke level berikutnya", "Rasakan bedanya"); generic hooks ("Stop scroll!", "POV:" without a specific situation); em dashes in ad copy. Full list in `practice-copywriting`.
- Descriptions add what the headline doesn't: a proof point, an objection handled ("Bayar di tempat"), the action.

## Drawing static ads on the canvas

Frames: feed 1080×1350 (4:5) as the default, square 1080×1080, story and Reels 1080×1920 with the top 250 and bottom 340 kept free of text and buttons. Same tokens and fonts as the site, so the ad and the landing page feel like one business.

Layouts worth drawing (from the source's 32; the ones that work without invented proof):

| Layout | Structure | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Headline statement | one dominant line (50-70% of the frame), the product photo, logo small | the words carry the idea |
| Product hero | the real product big on a calm ground, name and price | the product sells itself |
| Before / after | two panels with a clear divider, each labelled in customers' words | a visible transformation the person can show for real (check policy for beauty, health) |
| Problem / solution | the moment of friction, then the fix | problem-aware buyers |
| Feature spotlight | one part or ingredient big, with 2-3 short labels | product, food, craft |
| Numbered list | 3-5 short reasons or steps | services, explainers |
| FAQ card | one real customer question and its answer | answers a common objection from comments |
| Founder message | short plain note from the owner, their real photo | founder-led small business |
| With / without | two columns, plain facts | a clear practical difference |
| Simple guarantee | the guarantee in one line | only when the policy is real |
| Review card | one real review, verbatim, with permission | only with a real, permitted review |

Rules for the frame: one sentence or one photo dominates; line breaks keep phrases together; logo secondary; text readable at real phone size (check the frame at 390 wide, not just at 1080); no stock imagery competing with the sentence, no fake review stars or fake system notifications, no copied news mastheads. Photos are placeholders with their note (`pen-format`).

## Iterating with results

If the person has numbers (CTR, cost per order, messages received): find what the winners share (theme, structure, length), what losers share, then make variations that extend the winners and test one or two new angles. Change one thing per round and give each ad about 1,000 impressions before judging. Write the hypothesis so it can fail: "Sudut harga mengalahkan sudut antar-jemput dalam jumlah chat, untuk anak kos."
