# Using the person's own assets

Reuse what the person already has, their logo, photos, menu and earlier uploads, before you draw stand-ins, and never pretend a file exists.

The person's own material makes a design theirs faster than any style choice: the real logo sets the accent, the real shopfront photo sets the hero, the real menu sets the copy. Asking them to send it again wastes their goodwill.

## When it applies

- They refer to something they gave before: "logo yang tadi", "foto toko yang saya kirim", "menu di chat sebelumnya".
- You need a picture or a mark and the brief or the conversation already holds one.
- They say they prefer their own photos to stand-ins.

## The recipe

1. **Search tightly.** Look through the conversation and the files you were given for the kind you need: logo, a photo of the place, a product shot, a price list. Search by what it shows ("etalase roti", "logo hijau"), not "gambar".
2. **Pick by fit.** Prefer the largest, sharpest one with the right orientation for its slot (a wide shot for a 16:9 hero, a square for a product card). Note its real ratio and frame the placeholder to it.
3. **Place it.** The canvas can't hold an image (`pen-format`): draw the frame at the real size and ratio, fill it with the photo's dominant tone, and write the note inside naming the person's file: `Foto pemilik · etalase-depan.jpg · lebar, pagi`. Koda swaps in the file.
4. **Take its facts.** A logo gives the accent and maybe the heading face; a menu photo gives the prices and item names; a shopfront gives the street and the paint colour. Write them into your fact sheet.
5. **Report.** In the handoff, list each of the person's files and where it is used.

## Rules

- **Never invent a file name or path** the person didn't give. If nothing fits, say so and use a stand-in placeholder clearly marked as a stand-in (Taste: licensed stand-in photographs only when the client has none, never passed off as theirs).
- Their logo is used as given: no redrawing, recolouring or stretching it. If it only exists as a low-resolution photo, ask for the original file and note it.
- Their photos come first in the hero and the main sections; stand-ins fill only what they don't cover, and each stand-in note says "contoh, ganti dengan foto Anda".
- Don't place a photo that shows a customer's face, a car plate or a private document without the person confirming it may go online.
- When two files conflict (two logo versions, two price lists), ask which is current before using either.
