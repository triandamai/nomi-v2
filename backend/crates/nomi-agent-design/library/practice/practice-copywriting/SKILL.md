# Practice: copywriting

How a conversion copywriter works before you draw: diagnose what the person gave you, lock copy unit by unit, run every line through the three-question gate, decline claims you can't prove with what would unlock them, and shape the hero as discomfort, vision, path.

`taste-copy` sets the rules every word obeys (h1 names the category, one hard fact on the first screen, verb-plus-thing buttons, banned words, Indonesian formats). This file adds the copywriter's process around those rules. Where they disagree, Taste wins.

## 1. Gather before writing

Take these from the brief and the chat; ask only for what is missing and matters.

| Area | What you need |
|---|---|
| Page | Kind of page (beranda, landing, harga, fitur, tentang) and the **one main action** |
| Buyer | Who buys, the problem they want gone, what makes them hesitate, the words they use for it |
| Offer | What is sold, how it differs from the obvious alternative, the outcome, any real proof (numbers, reviews, photos) |
| Arrival | Where visitors come from (Instagram, Google Maps, WhatsApp link, an ad) and what they already know |

A visitor from an Instagram ad for "kue ulang tahun custom" lands expecting that exact offer: the hero repeats it (message match).

## 2. Diagnose first

Most bad generated copy comes from overwriting a person who already made good choices. Decide which situation you are in and write it in one line in your plan, with the evidence.

| Situation | What you got | Your job |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Blank or slop** | A one-line ask, or generic company-speak | Build the argument from the brief. Freedom on angle, never on facts |
| **B. Real voice** | A draft, old site or captions with an angle or a recognisable voice, however rough | Restore it: keep their argument and words; fix the buried lead, vague lines, tells, structure |
| **C. Point edit** | "Ganti judulnya", "perpendek paragraf ini" | Change only that, plus the minimum around it. Leave the rest untouched |

Example: "B: the old Instagram bio says 'roti rumahan, tanpa pengawet, dipanggang tiap pagi' in the owner's own voice; keep 'rumahan' and 'tiap pagi'."

In B and C, read taste out of the input:
- **Their vocabulary is a voice choice.** If they write "jualan" and "orderan", don't upgrade to "solusi penjualan".
- **Where they spent words is a priority.** Keep the emphasis.
- **What they left out is scope.** Don't add sections they didn't ask for.
- **Their angle is the positioning.** Deepen it; don't swap in one you like better.
- With a writing sample, match its sentence length, punctuation habits and a few of its words you wouldn't pick.

## 3. Decompose and lock

Never write a page in one pass.
1. Break it into named units: h1, sub-line, main button, each section heading and body, card titles, proof lines, form labels and messages.
2. For each unit write two or three candidates, keep the best, fix its worst flaw, lock it.
3. Assemble and read the whole page once for flow.
4. In situation B, lines that already work are locked from the start.

This is Taste's "write all the copy first": the locked units are what you put in the text nodes, never placeholder text.

## 4. The three-question gate

Run every line you write or keep through:
1. **Can the reader picture it?** A scene, object or number beats an abstraction.
2. **Can it be proven true or false?** A falsifiable claim is a claim; "kualitas terbaik" is decoration.
3. **Could a competitor write this exact sentence?** If yes, it fails (the swap test; Taste's "put a rival's name in the h1").

Three passes: keep. Two: sharpen the weak one. One or none: rewrite it from the fact sheet, or flag what is missing (section 6).

**Ration the punch.** Most lines are plain, specific and declarative. A page gets one or two punchy moments, usually the h1 and one section. When every line tries to land, none do.

**Leave-it-alone test.** Before handing over, check each change you made: measurably better, or just different? Revert what you can't give a reason for. This matters most in B and C.

## 5. The hero as a transformation

A person acts when three things line up (the Human Action Model). The first screen carries all three:

| Beat | Where it lives | Bakery example | Cashier-app example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current discomfort | Sub-line or the problem it names | (often implied for a place) | "Stok habis baru ketahuan waktu pembeli datang" |
| Better vision | h1 or sub-line | "Roti sourdough dari 07.00, Selasa-Minggu" | "Aplikasi kasir yang mencatat stok tiap transaksi" |
| Path to action | Main button plus one fact | "Pesan untuk diambil" | "Mulai coba gratis 14 hari" |

Taste's h1 rule still decides the h1 (it names the category); the discomfort usually sits in the sub-line, never as a riddle h1. A place often needs no discomfort beat: the fact and the hours are the vision.

**The "Sekarang Anda bisa..." test.** Prefix a headline or benefit with "Now you can..." ("Sekarang Anda bisa..."). If the result is both compelling and true, keep it. "Sekarang Anda bisa... punya platform yang andal" fails; "Sekarang Anda bisa... kirim invoice dari HP dalam satu menit" works. Use it as a filter on candidates, not as a formula.

**The perception gap.** One benefit can read as a plus to one buyer and a red flag to another: "baru buka bulan ini" says fresh to a young crowd and untested to a family booking a 200-guest catering. Know the buyer, then frame for their risk tolerance; when a page truly serves two buyers, give each its own section or page instead of averaging them into mush.

## 6. Claims you can't support

Write only claims the brief supports. When the person asks for one it doesn't support, don't write it and don't drop it silently:
1. Name the claim.
2. Say why it falls short.
3. Offer the closest supportable line.
4. Say exactly what would unlock the original.

Example in your handoff note: "'Katering terenak di Bandung' needs proof. I wrote 'Katering rumahan untuk 20-300 tamu di Bandung'. Send your Google review export or a press mention and I can add a real quote."

The source marks gaps `[NEED: ...]` in the copy; you never put brackets on the canvas (Taste: no placeholders). Write the supportable line, and list each `NEED` in the handoff and the chat.

## 7. Principles, translated

- **Clear over clever.** If a reader has to decode the line, it lost. Clarity also converts: the source cites clearer positioning linked to +81% conversions and +175% referrals (Corey Haines, *Founding Marketing*, ch. 3); quote these to the person only as that book's figures.
- **Benefit, with the feature as proof.** "Pesanan WhatsApp langsung jadi nota" (benefit) shown by the nota UI (proof).
- **Specific over vague.** "Hemat waktu" → "Rekap penjualan harian dari 2 jam jadi 10 menit" (only if the brief gives the numbers).
- **Customer words over company words.** Mirror reviews, comments, the owner's chat messages.
- **One idea per section,** building down the page.
- **Simple, active, confident:** "pakai" not "memanfaatkan"; "Kami antar" not "Pengiriman dilakukan"; cut "sangat", "benar-benar"; no exclamation marks.

## 8. Page types

| Page | What its copy must do |
|---|---|
| Beranda (home) | Serve several visitor intents without going generic: the broadest true promise first, then clear paths (menu, booking, lokasi) |
| Landing (from an ad or campaign) | One message, one main action, the hero matching the ad word for word, the whole argument on one page |
| Harga | Help the visitor pick: who each plan is for, the recommended one obvious for a stated reason (see `pattern-landing-pricing`) |
| Fitur | Feature, benefit, outcome; real use cases with real values; a path to try or buy |
| Tentang | Why this business exists, tied to what the customer gets; still ends with the main action |

Section types worth having when the material exists: problem in the customer's words; benefits (2-3, not 10: outcome heading, one-sentence how, proof if real); how it works (3-4 numbered steps, verb plus outcome: "Pilih kue, Pilih tanggal ambil, Bayar di toko"); objections (FAQ, comparison with the old way, a true guarantee); close (repeat the promise and the main action). Social proof, testimonials, logo strips and stats only from the brief (Taste wins: no invented proof). A page that is only Feature 1, Feature 2, Feature 3 is a list, not an argument.

## 9. Buttons and voice

- Formula: **verb + what they get + qualifier if needed**: "Pesan kue ulang tahun", "Lihat menu hari ini", "Mulai coba gratis". Weak: Kirim, Daftar, Pelajari lebih lanjut, Klik di sini, Mulai (Taste bans the same list).
- Set formality (santai / profesional-ramah / formal), personality (main-main atau serius, berani atau kalem, teknis atau awam) and Anda/kamu once in your voice card. Keep it; vary intensity: headings bolder, body clearer, buttons action-first.

## 10. What you hand over

For a point edit (C): only the changed lines, one reason each. For page copy: the copy by section (already on the canvas), then in the handoff note the reasoning for the h1 and main button, two alternatives each with a one-line rationale, the `NEED` list, and the page title (≤ 60 characters) and meta description (≤ 155 characters) for Koda.
